CAPTCHA / Bot check

Make visitors prove they are human before they reach a proxy rule. Pick a provider (Cloudflare Turnstile, Google reCAPTCHA, Friendly Captcha or the self-hosted Altcha), then customise how the challenge, failure and blocked pages look. The challenge also backs the Challenge action of the Security Center bot rules and the country challenge. Previews use the provider's public test keys and verify nothing.
