Privacy

IP addresses are personal data (for example under the GDPR). Only keep this list if you have a reason to, tell your visitors in your privacy policy, and keep the retention short. URL query strings are not stored unless you enable it below, requests from addresses on the statistics exclusion list are not recorded, and every record can be deleted from here.
